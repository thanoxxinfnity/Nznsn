# KHILAUNA NAGAR — Poori Kahani + Animation list

First-person horror, mobile. Tum NEHA ho. Dar = andhera + awaaz + intezaar (jump-scare kam).

---

## 1. Kahani ek nazar mein

"Rangeela Toys" company ne apne mazdooron ke liye ek poora shehar banaya — **Khilauna Nagar**. Factory ke neeche ek raaz tha: **"Yaadon ka Engine" (Memory Core)**. Company ne mazdooron ki yaadein, awaazein aur pyaar nikaal ke khilaunon mein bhar diye taaki khilaune "zinda" lagein aur bikein.

30 saal pehle ek raat Engine hadd se zyada chala. Poori aabaadi gayab ho gayi. Mazdoor khilaunon mein qaid ho gaye. Shehar ruk gaya.

**Neha** ki maa "Savitri" factory mein gudiya-painter thi. Wo bhi gayab ho gayi. 30 saal baad Neha ko maa ki aakhri chitthi milti hai:

> "Meri gudiya ko neeche mat le jaana."

Neha factory ke gate par pahunchti hai.

---

## 2. Act 1 — Factory (sikhne ka hissa)

- Raat, baarish, bijli band. Gate khulta hai (pehla darwaza).
- Torch milti hai. Batteri khatam hoti hai. **Roshni = surakshit, andhera = khatra.**
- Reception, assembly line, painting room. Har jagah khilaune, diary ke panne.
- **GUDIYA** pehli baar: seedhi khadi, ghoor rahi hai. Jab tum use nahi dekhti, wo paas aati hai.
- Pehla chase. Neha almari mein chhupti hai.
- Fuse-box se bijli chalu -> neeche jaane wali lift khulti hai.
- Pehli diary: "Engine ko roko... wo humari yaadein kha raha hai."

## 3. Act 2 — Shehar (4 mohalle)

Har mohalle ka apna monster, apni paheli, apni diary.

| Mohalla | Monster | Paheli / khauf |
|---|---|---|
| **School** | MASTER-JI puppet | Chup raho: wo class mein sawaal poochta hai, galat hilne par pakadta hai. Blackboard par code. |
| **Aspatal** | NURSE DOLL + DOCTOR KHILAUNA | Operation theatre, bijli band-chalu. Dono ek saath nahi dikhte. Dawai ki chaabi. |
| **Mela (Carnival)** | RINGMASTER + JESTER (Maskhara) | Mirror house, haunted house facade, jhoola. Jester hasta hai, tab tak safe. Hansi ruki = khatra. |
| **Metro** | CONDUCTOR puppet | Andheri surang, train ke dabbe, signal. Awaaz se hi ruk-chalna. |

Beech mein: **LAMBU KAKA** (3 meter lamba, button ki aankhein) shehar mein ghoomta hai. Awaaz se dhoondhta hai — daudogi to sunega.
**KHOKHLA** (teddy, monkey, clown, toy soldier ka jhund): bijli band hote hi chalte hain.

Har mohalle mein 3-4 diary/tape: sachchai jodti hai — company ne kya kiya, maa kahan gayi.

## 4. Act 3 — "Maa Ghar" (sabse neeche)

Metro se lift, neeche factory ke dil mein. Ek ghar jaisa kamra: jhoola, silai machine, maa ki bed, tasveerein.
**GUDIYA MAA** — sabse bada khilauna, Neha ki maa ki awaaz mein gaati hai: *"Meri bacchi aa gayi..."*
Neha ko pata chalta hai: **Gudiya = maa ki yaadon ka roop**. Maa ne khud ko Engine ke andar rakha taaki bachchon ko bacha sake.

Memory Core ke saamne **do raaste**:
- (A) Core band karo -> maa aazad, par factory/shehar toot jaata hai.
- (B) Gudiya Maa ke saath jao... Core ko chalu rakho, Neha uski jagah le leti hai (bura ant).

## 5. AAKHRI BHAAG — Factory se bhaago (final escape)

Jis bhi ant ko choono: **Engine phatne lagta hai.**
- Alarm, laal batti, bijli bhadakti hai, pipe phat-te hain, chhatein girti hain.
- Timer ya raasta ek ek karke band hota jaata hai.
- **Raasta:** Maa Ghar -> Boiler room -> Conveyor hall -> Loading dock -> Factory gate.
- **Peeche:** Khokhla ka jhund. **Aage:** Lambu Kaka aur tootay raaste, aag, giri hui chhat.
- Chhup ke ya daud ke bachna. Gate ke liye fuse + chaabi chahiye.
- Gate khulta hai -> subah ki roshni.

**Ant 1 (Azaadi):** Neha bahar nikalti hai. Peeche factory dhamake se dhah jaati hai. Aasmaan mein maa ki halki awaaz: *"Chalo, ghar chalein."*
**Ant 2 (Bura):** Neha mud jaati hai... Gudiya Maa Neha ka haath thaam leti hai. Screen kaali. Naye khilaune ke roop mein Neha ki aankhein khulti hain.

---

## 6. First-person hath (zaroori)
Poore asli hath, **har ungli alag** (5 ungliyan, har ek mein 3 jod). Torch pakadna, darwaza dhakelna, chaabi ghumana, almari kholna, mutthi, ungli se ishaara.

## 7. Darwaze
Saaf hinge par khulte hain: dheere/tez dhakka, chaabi wale, almari, lift, shutter. Awaaz + halka creak. Koi clipping nahi.

---

## 8. ANIMATION LIST (jo chahiye)

### A. Player (first-person hath, rig: 5 ungliyan x 3 jod)
- idle (saans lena, halka hilna)
- walk / run / crouch (hath halke hilte)
- torch pakadna, torch jalana/bujhana, torch hilana
- darwaza dhakelna (ek hath / do hath)
- chaabi lagana-ghumana
- almari/drawer kholna, andar ghusna (chhupna)
- cheez uthana (diary, battery, chaabi)
- dar se kaanpna, girna, uthna
- lift ka button dabana

### B. Gudiya (M01) — chhoti, patli, chini-mitti
- idle (statue jaisi khadi)
- "hilti jab dekha na jaye": ruk-ruk ke chalna (frozen poses ke beech teleport-jaisa jhatka)
- tezi se daudna (chase), sir ka tedha mudna
- hamla (jhapatna), girna, ubharna
- hansna/ya sir ghumana (creepy)

### C. Lambu Kaka (M05) — 3m
- idle (jhoolna), lamba chalna (dheere), jhukna
- sunna (sir tedha), daudna, lambe haath phailana, pakadna
- darwaze se jhukkar guzarna

### D. Khokhla (Teddy M02, Monkey M06, Clown M07, Toy Soldier M08)
- idle, ghisatna/chalna, daudna, jhapatna
- Monkey: cymbal bajana. Clown: hansi ke saath hilna. Soldier: march.

### E. Master-ji, Nurse Doll, Doctor Khilauna, Conductor, Ringmaster, Jester
- idle, walk, run (chase), search (idhar-udhar dekhna), attack, hit-reaction
- Master-ji: chalk se likhna, sawaal poochna
- Nurse/Doctor: injection uthana, hasna
- Jester: hasna (loop), nachna
- Conductor: seeti, signal dena

### F. Gudiya Maa (final boss, bada)
- idle (gaana gungunana), chalna, hath phailana, dahaadna, hamla, girna

### G. Props / environment
- darwaze (khulna, band hona, atakna), almari, lift, shutter, gate
- fuse-box switch, jhoola, merry-go-round, train signal, boiler dial
- Engine phatna: pipe, bijli, chhat girna (final bhaag)

### Format (kripya)
- Humanoid: **T-pose ya A-pose**, standard humanoid skeleton (Mixamo jaisa). Har animation alag clip, loop wale (idle/walk/run) seamless.
- Animation ka naam saaf: `Gudiya_Idle`, `Gudiya_Chase`, `Player_DoorPush` ...
- Hath: alag armature (5 ungliyan, har ek 3 bone).
- Export: GLB ya FBX. Frame rate 30.
